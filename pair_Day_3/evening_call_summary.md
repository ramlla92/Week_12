We had the evening call and discussed the explainer and evaluation ideas in detail, although we had to wrap the discussion up earlier than planned. The main focus was improving the clarity of the concept breakdown and making the practical evaluation strategies easier to understand.

We discussed how shortcut learning can appear in preference-tuned classifiers and why diverse explanation styles, hard negative samples, and held-out evaluation formats are important for testing genuine reasoning behavior.
