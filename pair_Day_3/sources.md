## Papers

1. Geirhos et al., “Shortcut Learning in Deep Neural Networks”  
https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.07780

This paper explains how deep neural networks often learn superficial correlations and shortcut patterns instead of the intended reasoning behavior. It helped ground the idea that a preference-tuned classifier may memorize explanation styles rather than learn the true classification boundary.

---

2. Rafailov et al., “Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model”  
https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290

This paper explains how preference optimization works and how chosen/rejected response pairs shape model behavior during post-training. It helped connect preference-pair structure to classifier calibration and reasoning quality.
