## Papers

1. Efron & Tibshirani — *An Introduction to the Bootstrap*  
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4899-4541-9

This foundational work explains bootstrap resampling, confidence intervals, and how bootstrap distributions approximate sampling distributions from observed data.

---

2. Chen et al. — *Evaluating Large Language Models Trained on Code*  
https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03374

This paper explains Pass@k estimation and statistical evaluation methods commonly used in modern LLM benchmark reporting.

---

## Concepts / Methods

- Paired Bootstrap Resampling
- Confidence Interval Estimation
- Sampling Distribution Approximation
- Monte Carlo Variance
- Exchangeability Assumption

These concepts were used to understand why paired bootstrap is appropriate for shared-task evaluations and how confidence intervals and p-values should be interpreted in benchmark reporting.
